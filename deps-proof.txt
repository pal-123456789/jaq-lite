Package entries in Cargo.lock: 1. The one entry is jaq-lite itself,
which is what an empty dependency graph looks like in a lock file: cargo
records the workspace member and has nothing else to record.

--offline proves nothing was fetched to make this build. --locked proves the
lock file was not rewritten to make it succeed. Together they rule out the
two ways an accidental dependency could hide.
